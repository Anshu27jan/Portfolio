Background

Background & Problem Space

Tour & Travels was designed as a unified digital platform that simplifies trip planning, package discovery, booking management, and travel operations through an intuitive and user-friendly experience.

Travel planning often involves switching between multiple platforms for discovering destinations, comparing packages, managing bookings, and tracking travel-related activities. This fragmented experience creates friction for both travelers and travel operators, making the overall journey inefficient and time-consuming.

The Problem

Why a Design System?

A scalable design system was established to ensure visual consistency, faster design iterations, efficient developer handoff, and a seamless experience across all user touchpoints.

  • Consistent experience across web and mobile platforms
  • Faster design and development workflows
  • Reusable UI components and patterns
  • Improved scalability for future features
  • Better collaboration between design and development teams

Discovery

Key Challenges

Through research and analysis, several usability and business challenges were identified:

  • Users struggled to compare destinations, packages, and travel options efficiently
  • Travel information was scattered across multiple screens and workflows
  • Booking and approval processes lacked clarity and visibility
  • Mobile and desktop experiences required better consistency
  • Users needed quicker access to reports, analytics, and booking status
Strategy

Design Objectives

  • Simplify the end-to-end travel planning experience
  • Create intuitive navigation across mobile and web platforms
  • Improve booking visibility and travel management workflows
  • Provide clear access to analytics, reports, and operational insights
  • Build a scalable and responsive design system
