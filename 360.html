Background

Background & Problem Space

Electrical and gas engineers in the UK often rely on multiple tools, paperwork, and disconnected systems to create, manage, and track compliance certificates. This process can be time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to manage across multiple customers, job sites, and certification types.

360 Connect Certificate was designed as a centralized digital platform that simplifies certificate management, streamlines field operations, and helps engineers maintain compliance while improving operational efficiency.

  • Digital certificate creation and management
  • Customer and site management
  • Job tracking and field operations
  • Compliance and safety documentation
The Problem

Why a Design System?

The platform includes multiple modules across web and mobile experiences, ranging from certificate generation and customer management to job tracking and reporting. A scalable design system was essential to maintain consistency and support future product growth.

The design system helped create a unified experience while reducing design and development effort across the platform.

  • Consistent UI across web and mobile
  • Reusable components and design patterns
  • Faster product development cycles
  • Improved developer handoff process
  • Better scalability for future modules

Discovery

Key Challenges

The platform needed to support engineers working in both office and field environments while handling complex certification workflows and regulatory requirements.

  • Simplifying complex certification processes
  • Managing multiple certificate types and workflows
  • Supporting mobile-first usage in the field
  • Maintaining compliance and data accuracy
Strategy

Design Objectives

The goal was to create an intuitive platform that allows engineers to manage certificates, customers, and jobs efficiently while ensuring compliance with industry standards.

  • Simplify certificate creation and submission
  • Improve field productivity through mobile workflows
  • Reduce administrative workload
  • Enable quick access to customer and site information
  • Improve visibility of certificate status and progress
