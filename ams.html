Background

Background & Problem Space

The Sports Authority of India (SAI) required a centralized platform to manage athlete development, performance tracking, wellness monitoring, nutrition planning, and sports science data across multiple training centers and programs. Existing workflows relied on fragmented systems and manual processes, making it difficult to maintain consistency, visibility, and long-term athlete records.

AMS (Athlete Management System) was designed to create a unified digital ecosystem that enables athletes, coaches, sports scientists, and administrators to collaborate through a structured, data-driven platform.

  • Athlete performance tracking
  • Training and workload management
  • Nutrition and wellness monitoring
  • Medical and laboratory reporting
The Problem

Why a Design System?

AMS serves multiple user groups across web and mobile platforms, each with unique workflows and responsibilities. Maintaining consistency across dashboards, forms, reports, and athlete management modules required a scalable design system.

The design system ensured a unified user experience while improving collaboration between design, development, and sports administration teams.

  • Consistent user experience across modules
  • Reusable components and patterns
  • Faster feature development and scalability
  • Improved design-to-development handoff
  • Better accessibility and usability standards

Discovery

Key Challenges

Designing AMS involved managing complex workflows and large volumes of athlete-related data while ensuring usability for multiple stakeholders.

  • Managing diverse user roles and permissions
  • Presenting complex sports science data clearly
  • Maintaining long-term athlete records and history
  • Simplifying extensive forms and data-entry workflows
  • Supporting both web and mobile experiences
Strategy

Design Objectives

The primary goal was to create a user-friendly platform that simplifies athlete management while enabling informed decision-making through data and insights.

  • Centralize athlete information and performance data
  • Improve visibility of training, wellness, and nutrition metrics
  • Simplify reporting and assessment workflows
  • Enable role-based access for different stakeholders
  • Create intuitive dashboards for quick decision-making
