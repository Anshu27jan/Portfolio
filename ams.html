Background

Background & Problem Space

Fit India was envisioned as a unified fitness platform that simplifies health tracking, workout planning, and progress monitoring while encouraging users to stay committed to their wellness goals. Through user research, we identified several key challenges:.

  • Lack of motivation and consistency in maintaining fitness routines
  • Complex user experiences that discourage long-term engagement
  • Difficulty tracking workouts, goals, and overall progress in one place
  • Limited personalization based on individual fitness levels and objectives
The Problem

Why a Design System?

As Fit India evolved from a simple fitness concept into a comprehensive health and wellness platform, maintaining consistency across screens became increasingly challenging. Multiple user journeys—including workout tracking, goal setting, progress monitoring, nutrition guidance, and profile management—required a unified experience.

  • Users struggled to navigate between different fitness features due to inconsistent layouts and interactions
  • Similar actions looked and behaved differently across screens, creating confusion and increasing learning time
  • New features were taking longer to design and develop because patterns were not standardized
  • Inconsistent visual elements reduced user trust and weakened the overall product identity
  • Maintaining accessibility, responsiveness, and usability across devices became increasingly difficult

Discovery

Key Challenges

Research revealed that users wanted a simple, motivating, and goal-oriented platform that could help them track progress, stay accountable, and build sustainable fitness habits without unnecessary complexity.

  • Users felt overwhelmed by too many features and choices, leading to decision fatigue and reduced engagement
  • Limited visibility of fitness progress and achievements made it difficult for users to stay motivated over time
  • Inconsistent navigation and user flows created friction when accessing workouts, goals, and activity tracking
  • Lack of personalized recommendations resulted in generic experiences that failed to match individual fitness levels
  • Users struggled to understand the impact of their daily activities due to unclear progress indicators and feedback mechanisms
Strategy

Design Objectives

Based on our research findings, we focused on creating a fitness experience that was simple, motivating, and user-centered. The strategy was built around reducing complexity, increasing user engagement, and helping individuals stay consistent with their fitness journey. By prioritizing clarity, personalization, and progress visibility, we aimed to create an experience that users could easily adopt and maintain over the long term.

  • Simplify the fitness journey by reducing unnecessary complexity and cognitive load
  • Create clear and intuitive navigation for workouts, goals, activity tracking, and progress monitoring
  • Increase user motivation through visible achievements, milestones, and progress indicators
  • Deliver personalized fitness recommendations based on user goals, activity levels, and preferences

Solution

What I Designed

The platform was designed to reduce friction, improve accessibility, and provide users with clear visibility into their fitness journey while maintaining a consistent and engaging experience across all touchpoints.

  • Personalized onboarding experience to understand user goals, fitness levels, and preferences
  • Goal-setting and progress tracking dashboards with clear visual indicators and achievement milestones
  • Workout discovery and activity management flows that make finding and starting fitness routines effortless
  • Personalized recommendations based on user interests, activity history, and fitness objectives
