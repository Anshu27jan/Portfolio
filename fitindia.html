Background

Background & Problem Space

Sprinklr's product suite had grown fast. But design wasn't keeping up — different teams built components independently, each with their own interpretation of colour, spacing, and interaction.

This created significant friction across the lifecycle, especially during:

  • Designer onboarding — no single reference to work from
  • Developer handoff — components rebuilt multiple times across codebases
  • Cross-team collaboration — no shared language, constant rework
  • Product consistency — users experienced jarring visual differences across the same platform
The Problem

Why a Design System?

A full audit revealed the scale of the problem. Siloed design had caused chaos hidden behind a product:

  • 200+ components built in isolation across 6 product teams
  • Higher cognitive load due to multiple inconsistencies per page
  • Friction when onboarding new designers was significantly higher
  • Only ~40% of released pages used consistent token values
  • Engineers couldn't share active components — leading to duplication and slowdown
  • Revenue leakage due to poor clarity and lack of shared decisions

Discovery

Key Challenges

  • Decision fatigue caused by fragmented systems and repeated choices
  • Low visibility of high-intent sign-off efforts across teams
  • Lack of flexibility in layout and structure for the design system
  • Friction when users faced unexpected rejection of component updates
  • Lack of visual cues after applying tokens — leading to confusion and slowdown
  • Revenue leakage due to poor clarity, decisions and deletions
Strategy

Design Objectives

  • Reduce cognitive load and friction on critical decision points
  • Improve clarity, transparency, and confidence in selection
  • Make comparisons quick and intuitive with a hierarchy-first layout
  • Increase component adoption as a key driver of system health
  • Increase funnel momentum with outstanding lender visibility and consistent token use

Solution

What I Designed

I redesigned the entire design foundation for Sprinklr — covering both the component system and the operational layer that makes it sustainable.

  • Unified component library — single Figma file, 80 components, all states, all sizes, all variants
  • Enabled quick selection by allowing clear and easily performing expected interactions
  • Enabled lender discovery through high-visibility business channel CTAs and urgency cues
  • Verified flexibility in set-up efforts, allowing merchants to adjust loan amounts and tenure
  • 1.8% users modified their loan amount from default — 14% applied highest tenure despite shorter being default
  • Ensured post-application transparency by outlining active loan details, reducing confusion
